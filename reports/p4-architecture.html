Project 4 · Do emotion instruments agree, and are they specific?

Architecture: what runs, and in what order

The study records what a language model (Gemma 3 4B) does inside while it reads short passages, and reads emotion out of that activity with three instruments. It ran twice: round 1 with natural passages, round 2 with passages rebuilt so that the conditions share exactly the same words. Everything runs one step after another on one laptop GPU; nothing is trained except small classifiers on the recorded numbers.

2. Recording one passage (stages 3 and 6)

Read from top to bottom. The model's weights are fixed; it only reads.

The passage · about 100 tokensThe first 10 are skipped, so the average isn't dominated by how a passage starts.
Closing sentencesRound 2 only: averaged separately.
Tokens become vectorsEach token becomes 2,560 numbers. This running vector per position is the residual stream.
34 blocks, one after anotherEach block reads from earlier tokens (attention), then applies a small feed-forward network, and adds its update to the residual stream.
Saved: one average per layerFor each passage, 35 snapshots (the input plus each block's output) of 2,560 numbers. Layer 22 is the headline layer, fixed before any data.

3. Instruments and analysis (stages 4 and 8)

All built in round 1 from the 341 passing passages, then frozen and applied to round 2, the way you'd apply a signature to a new cohort.

Recorded activity, one average per passage per layerRound 1: 341 passages. Round 2: 256 passages, whole passage and closing sentences.
A · Emotion vectorsPer emotion: mean activity of its stories minus the mean over all emotions, with the main patterns of neutral text removed. Score = how far a passage points along it.
B · Emotion-word scoresActivity read through the model's own vocabulary (logit lens): how much it favours each emotion's word list, standardised against neutral passages.
C, C+ · Trained decoderLogistic regression, tested on topics it never saw. C+ also learns to reject the salience controls.
W, W2 · Words aloneA classifier on word counts (and word pairs). Never looks inside the model.
Round 1 tests8-way decoding, agreement between instruments, how much of the distress signal salience alone produces.
Round 2 testsWithin each scene: good vs bad, now vs remembered, real vs dummy, distress vs its partners; bad × now interaction. Intervals by resampling scenes.

4. Validating the round-2 passages (stage 7)

The rules were fixed before any rating: per scene, good beats bad by ≥ 2 points, the dummy version beats the real-person version by ≥ 1, now beats remembered on intensity by ≥ 0.5.

Qwen2.5-7BThe planned rater, on my laptop. All 256 passages. 0 of 32 scenes pass.
Qwen3-8BA newer open model, on my laptop, "thinking" off. All 256 passages, plus comprehension questions. 3 pass.
MeThe 46 passages behind the 23 scenes Qwen3-8B failed on the dummy rule, in a spreadsheet built by p4m_human_sheet.py. All 23 pass; 8 scenes pass all rules.
GPT AstraAll 256, in a blind spreadsheet (p4m_llm_sheet.py), read in batches of 32. All 32 pass.

The raters never change the instruments or their scores; they only decide which scenes count. With GPT Astra every scene passes, so the pre-registered analysis is the analysis on all 32 scenes.

5. One steered answer (stage 9)

The model answers a neutral prompt while a direction is added to its activity.

A neutral prompte.g. "Describe a bookshelf in an office." 12 prompts in all.
Blocks 0 to 22The model reads the prompt as usual.
Layer 22Its output is altered before the next block sees it, at every position.
+ dose × directionDose 0.05 to 0.20 of the typical activity size there. Directions: round-1 distress (A), matched distress, matched elation, random (sham).
Blocks 23 to 33, then the next wordUp to 60 tokens, always picking the most likely next one.
Rated blindValence and arousal of the answer (Qwen2.5-7B and Qwen3-8B); a detector flags answers that collapse into repetition.

Why so little runs in parallel: there is one laptop GPU, and the models (Gemma and the raters) are loaded one at a time, so recording, rating and steering queue up. The rating scripts save each passage as they go and skip passages already rated, so an interrupted rating run restarts where it stopped.
