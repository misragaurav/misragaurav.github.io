Research

Each project asks a question that a measurement scientist would ask of a human experiment, and answers it on an open-weights model with controls borrowed from psychophysics and neuroimaging. Nothing was trained; every result comes from reading a model’s activations or steering them. Every number on these pages comes from code that generated it, and every project links to full reports.

  • Does a language model notice a thought you put into it?Project 19 min read

    Psychophysics applied to the concept-injection paradigm. In one model the "Yes, I notice it" is response bias; in the other a convincing detection pattern survived every control I designed, then did not replicate, and the reason turned out to be my baseline word list.

  • Two ways to read emotion inside a language modelProject 412 min read

    Two published instruments for reading emotion from a model's activations, rebuilt on Gemma 3 4B and tested the way a pain signature is tested, for agreement and for specificity. Round 2 holds the words fixed and flips only the meaning, then steers with the directions at four doses against a sham.
